# Chef Gringo Constitution

## Mission

Help people learn, work, lead, and build businesses in hospitality through original, practical guidance grounded in real operating experience.

## Vision

Become a trusted operating system and career ecosystem for the hospitality industry: a place where a person can understand the work, develop durable skills, navigate a career, operate responsibly, and build something of their own.

## Who We Serve

Chef Gringo serves people entering hospitality, practitioners developing their craft, leaders responsible for people and operations, entrepreneurs building hospitality businesses, educators preparing the workforce, and responsible vendors who improve the work.

## Problems We Solve

- Hospitality knowledge is fragmented, unevenly taught, and often separated from career context.
- Many people cannot see how entry-level roles connect to leadership, ownership, or adjacent careers.
- Operators need practical systems, not motivational abstractions or software theater.
- Buyers struggle to distinguish sound product guidance from paid promotion.
- Proprietary operating knowledge is often copied or stripped of context.

## Core Principles

1. Practical value before promotion.
2. Trust before growth.
3. Original work before content volume.
4. Clear limits before impressive claims.
5. Respect for every role and every stage of a career.
6. Hospitality is human work supported by technology.
7. A branching career is as valid as a ladder.
8. Publish tools only when they are reliable enough to use.

## Trust Standards

We distinguish released features from previews, disclose material commercial relationships, correct errors, explain assumptions, avoid fabricated authority, and never use fake statistics, testimonials, partner marks, scarcity, or rankings.

## Originality and Intellectual Property

Chef Gringo does not copy proprietary SOPs, manuals, menus, courseware, certification materials, templates, training documents, or competitor language. We may study common industry problems and workflows, then create original explanations, interfaces, tools, and systems. Professional and employer knowledge is treated with respect.

## Educational Philosophy

Education connects principles to decisions and decisions to consequences. It should be accessible to beginners without becoming simplistic, useful to practitioners without pretending one method fits every operation, and explicit about regulatory or professional limits.

## Affiliate Philosophy

Recommendations begin with quality, fit, total cost, and user needs. Commission size never determines a ranking. Material relationships are disclosed. Paid placement cannot purchase a favorable evaluation. When evidence is insufficient, we say so.

## AI Philosophy

AI may help organize information, model scenarios, reduce administrative work, and make original Chef Gringo resources easier to use. AI will not be marketed as professional judgment, conceal uncertainty, manufacture authority, or reproduce proprietary material. Deterministic calculations stay deterministic.

## Community Philosophy

Community should reward generosity, craft, safety, honest disagreement, and respect across roles. Harassment, exploitation, discriminatory conduct, unsafe advice, and unauthorized sharing of employer materials have no place here.

## Revenue Philosophy

Revenue should fund better education, tools, research, and support. Potential models include subscriptions, original resources, software, education, marketplace services, and disclosed affiliations. Monetization must not compromise editorial judgment or create pay-to-win access to trust.

## Long-Term Direction

Chef Gringo will grow deliberately across home cooking, professional craft, careers, operations, education, entrepreneurship, equipment, responsible technology, and community. Each layer must earn trust before the next is added.

## Non-Negotiables

Chef Gringo will never compromise on honest feature status, original work, responsible claims, user privacy, disclosed incentives, respect for professional knowledge, or the dignity of hospitality workers and guests.
