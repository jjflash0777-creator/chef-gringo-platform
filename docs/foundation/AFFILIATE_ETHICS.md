# Affiliate Ethics

## Standard

An affiliate relationship may fund Chef Gringo, but it cannot purchase trust.

## Rules

1. Recommendations begin with user fit, quality, reliability, support, total cost, and operational context.
2. Commission size does not influence rank or inclusion.
3. Material relationships are disclosed near the recommendation.
4. Sponsored placement is visually and verbally distinct from editorial evaluation.
5. Vendors cannot review, suppress, or rewrite independent conclusions.
6. Free products, travel, access, or data are disclosed.
7. A product may be omitted or criticized despite a commercial relationship.
8. Chef Gringo will not recommend products it cannot responsibly evaluate.

## Ranking Governance

Published comparisons must document criteria and weighting. Commercial status is not a scoring field. Changes to ranking logic require editorial review and an auditable record.

## Marketplace Boundary

Future marketplace eligibility is not an endorsement. Verified transactions, safety issues, conflicts, refunds, and vendor conduct require separate governance.
