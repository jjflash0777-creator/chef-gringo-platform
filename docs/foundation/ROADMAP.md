# Roadmap

## Foundation Sprint 01 — Public Trust Layer

- Constitution and platform architecture
- Brand and design tokens
- Public landing, vision, founder, policy, and early-access pages
- Provider-neutral analytics
- Waitlist adapter with honest unconfigured state
- Accessibility, responsive navigation, tests, and production build

## Recommended Sprint 02 — Durable Early Access

Implement one consent-aware waitlist integration end to end: provider configuration, verified success, double opt-in where appropriate, abuse controls, privacy retention rules, export/deletion operations, and analytics confirmation without transmitting unnecessary personal data.

## Subsequent Gates

1. Audience research and taxonomy validation
2. Original editorial workflow and source governance
3. One complete learning pathway
4. One reliable operator tool family
5. Identity and progress only when personalization earns its cost
6. Recommendations only after affiliate governance is operational
7. Payments only after legal, tax, accounting, security, and refund readiness
8. Community only after moderation and safety systems exist

No phase should be accelerated by labeling a preview as a product.
