# Audience Architecture

Chef Gringo organizes people by current context, desired progress, and operating responsibility—not by status.

| Audience | Immediate needs | Likely next value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home cooks | Technique, confidence, adaptable food | Culinary foundations |
| Students | Career visibility, skill practice, workplace expectations | Learning paths and first-role preparation |
| Dishwashers | Kitchen rhythm, safety, advancement visibility | Prep-readiness roadmap |
| Prep cooks | Organization, knife skills, production | Line-cook development |
| Line cooks | Station execution, consistency, communication | Sous-chef readiness |
| Sous chefs | Shift leadership, training, systems | Executive leadership |
| Executive chefs | Financial and people leadership | Multi-site or ownership paths |
| Culinary directors | Complex operations, compliance, resident experience | Regional leadership and tools |
| Servers | Service craft, sales ethics, career options | Fine dining or management |
| Bartenders | Beverage craft, safety, economics | Bar leadership or ownership |
| Baristas | Coffee craft, workflow, guest service | Café leadership or roasting |
| Sommeliers | Study, service, program design | Beverage direction and education |
| Dining-room managers | People, service systems, guest recovery | General or multi-unit management |
| Food-truck owners | Mobile workflow, equipment, permits, unit economics | Scaling and diversification |
| Caterers | Sales, logistics, production, events | Systems and profitable growth |
| Restaurant owners | Strategy, finance, people, risk | Resilient operations and expansion |
| Senior-living operators | Resident-centered quality and operational complexity | Specialized tools and leadership |
| Healthcare dining professionals | Safety, service, production, clinical boundaries | Specialized education |
| Hospitality educators | Current, original, practical teaching resources | Curriculum collaboration |
| Vendors and suppliers | Responsible access to qualified buyers | Governed marketplace participation |

## Cross-Audience Rules

- Never imply that every person wants management or ownership.
- Treat front- and back-of-house roles with equal dignity.
- Make prerequisites and limitations visible.
- Separate beginner explanation from professional or regulated guidance.
- Allow users to follow multiple pathways and change direction.
