# Product Architecture

This document defines future platform pillars. Foundation Sprint 01 builds only the public marketing layer, early-access workflow, policy pages, and internal architecture.

## Future Pillars

1. **Home Cooking** — Practical culinary foundations and adaptable everyday food.
2. **Culinary Arts** — Technique, ingredient knowledge, production, and craft development.
3. **Professional Kitchens** — Systems for quality, safety, consistency, labor, cost, and execution.
4. **Fine Dining** — Service standards, dining-room craft, guest experience, and leadership.
5. **Wine and Beverage** — Wine, beer, spirits, cocktails, responsible service, and beverage economics.
6. **Coffee and Espresso** — Preparation, sensory development, equipment, café workflow, and operations.
7. **Food Safety and Certifications** — Preparation pathways and links to authoritative certification providers.
8. **Careers and Jobs** — Role exploration, workplace fit, career roadmaps, preparation, and eventually responsible job discovery.
9. **Hospitality Academy** — Structured learning paths and assessed original curricula.
10. **Senior Living and Healthcare Dining** — Resident-centered service, complex operations, and careful clinical boundaries.
11. **Food Trucks and Mobile Hospitality** — Concept design, permitting research, equipment, workflow, and mobile economics.
12. **Catering and Events** — Sales, planning, production, logistics, service, and post-event analysis.
13. **Hospitality Entrepreneurship** — Concept validation, unit economics, opening systems, finance literacy, and growth.
14. **Equipment and Marketplace** — Fit-based research, comparisons, disclosed affiliations, and eventually transactions.
15. **Chef Gringo Pro** — Advanced operating tools, templates, team workflows, and professional resources.
16. **Chef Gringo AI** — Carefully bounded assistance over original resources and user-authorized operational context.
17. **Community** — Moderated peer learning, mentorship, professional discussion, and local connection.

## Shared Platform Capabilities

- Content and curriculum metadata
- Search and taxonomy
- Accounts, preferences, and consent
- Progress and credential records
- Jobs and organization profiles
- Tool calculation engines
- Commerce and subscriptions
- Marketplace and vendor governance
- Analytics, experimentation, and privacy controls
- Moderation, safety, and audit trails

## Build Sequence

Marketing foundation precedes durable waitlist storage. Durable identity precedes personalized learning. Editorial governance precedes recommendations. A transactional marketplace follows, rather than leads, trust development.
