# Brand Foundation

## Positioning

Chef Gringo helps people learn, work, lead, and build businesses in hospitality.

## Brand Promise

Real experience translated into practical next steps.

## Character

Professional, warm, experienced, modern, ambitious, human, culinary, and trustworthy. Confident without swagger. Encouraging without pretending the work is easy.

## Voice

- Lead with the useful point.
- Use industry language when it adds precision; explain it when the audience may be new.
- Respect every role.
- Prefer concrete verbs and honest status labels.
- Use restrained humor, never contempt.
- Say “in development” instead of implying availability.

## Visual System

- Warm canvas and paper surfaces communicate hospitality and approachability.
- Deep night and forest tones provide professional authority.
- Brick red signals action; gold is a restrained craft accent.
- Editorial serif display type pairs with plain, highly readable sans-serif body type.
- Structure comes from spacing, borders, and typography rather than decorative gradients or restaurant clichés.

## Prohibited Patterns

Chef hats as a default identity, cartoon mascots, AI glow effects, fake partner walls, stock claims, generic restaurant templates, dense affiliate grids, manufactured scarcity, and exaggerated founder mythology.

## Core Language

- Build Your Future in Hospitality.
- Learn. Work. Lead. Build.
- Hospitality, from first shift to ownership.
- Practical value before promotion.
