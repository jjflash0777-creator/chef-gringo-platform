# IDDSI Pilot Risk Review

## Applied rubric

- **Low:** Failure primarily affects efficiency, appearance, workflow quality, or consistency.
- **Medium:** Failure may affect acceptance, documentation, sanitation process reliability, or controlled production, but the step is not itself the final safety or prescribed-texture control.
- **High:** Failure may materially affect resident safety, prescribed-texture conformity, allergen control, time/temperature control, resident identity, or release of an unverified product.

Risk is classified by credible consequence, not by how easy the step appears.

## High-risk gate assessment

High-risk steps: **1, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 12**.

All eight have:

- a specific measurable check;
- a corrective action;
- an explicit stop/escalation boundary;
- one or more step-level evidence links.

All eight still require qualified review and verified evidence. A draft source link is coverage for editorial preparation, not publication readiness.
