# Chef Gringo Campaign Landing Template v1

Status: APPROVED FOUNDATION
Reference implementation: `/go/toast`
First campaign: Toast — Independent Restaurant

## Purpose
Campaign landing pages are marketing and conversion destinations. They are not replacements for Chef Gringo's main intelligence product.

Main site: intelligence + trust + decisions.
Campaign pages: specific audience + specific pain + specific promise + qualified commercial action.

## Permanent structure
1. Campaign/audience-specific hero
2. One clear pain and promise
3. One primary commercial CTA
4. Verified current offer when one exists
5. Visual translation of the partner into the customer's workflow
6. Good fit / compare first honesty block
7. One meaningful limitation or tradeoff
8. Route back into Chef Gringo intelligence for uncertain users
9. Focused final offer/CTA
10. Clear, conspicuous referral/affiliate disclosure
11. Analytics on campaign view and outbound merchant action

## Design rules
- Campaign pages may visually differ from the main site but must remain recognizably Chef Gringo.
- No sprawling primary-site navigation.
- No unrelated marketplace products.
- Strong photography/video over walls of copy.
- One dominant CTA; secondary actions must support the buying decision.
- Mobile is a first-class layout.
- Use partner-approved assets when permitted; do not fabricate product imagery or claims.

## Integrity rules
- Never recommend a partner because commission is higher.
- No invented savings, conversion rates, testimonials, urgency, pricing, or product claims.
- Do not copy merchant marketing copy as the page's substance.
- Alternatives and limitations may be shown even when they reduce conversion.
- Commercial relationship must be disclosed near the commercial action.
- Offer language must be grounded in current partner terms/materials.
