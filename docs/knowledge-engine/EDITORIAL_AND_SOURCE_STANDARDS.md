# Editorial and Source Standards

## Originality and copyright

Chef Gringo recipe instructions, explanations, troubleshooting, and summaries must be written originally. A third-party chef interpretation may identify the creator and summarize a distinguishing approach in original language. Do not reproduce ingredient lists, step sequences, paywalled text, photographs, or substantial expressive passages without authorization.

Authorized links should point to the creator or publisher’s canonical source. A link is not permission to copy. Images require rights metadata and useful alt text.

## Claims and attribution

- Prefer primary sources and reputable scholarly or institutional sources.
- Attribute contested history and label uncertainty in the same context as the claim.
- Record publisher, URL, access date, and a note explaining what the source supports.
- Separate culinary convention from historical fact and personal preference.
- Restaurant menus, prices, retailer inventory, and service availability are time-sensitive and need a checked-at date.

## Review states

`source-ready` means the structure can accept evidence but final citations may be pending. `reviewed` means an identified review scope has been completed. `verified` is reserved for content whose material claims and rights status have passed the required review.

## Safety and judgment

Recipes are educational. Dietary needs and allergens require label and individual verification. Nutrition estimates must name their method and limitations. Production scaling must warn about batch size, equipment capacity, time and temperature controls, egg handling, holding, and discard policies.
