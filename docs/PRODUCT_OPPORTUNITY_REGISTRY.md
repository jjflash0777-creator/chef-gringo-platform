**Status:** Internal product-opportunity memory / implementation reference  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-08  
**Branch:** `docs/operating-system-architecture-v2`  
**Purpose:** Preserve high-fit product lanes and candidate products discovered for Chef Gringo so future Product Harvest, Marketplace, content, campaign, and revenue implementations can reuse them without relying on chat memory.

## Operating rules

- This registry records **candidates and product lanes**, not automatic endorsements.
- A product may only become a public recommendation after evidence, fit, current availability, and commercial-route checks are complete.
- Customer/operator value is evaluated before commission economics.
- Commercial relationship status must come from the partner/commercial source of truth, not from this registry.
- Prices, commission rates, cookie windows, and availability are time-sensitive and must be re-verified before publication or implementation.
- A product appearing in Impact Product Marketplace does **not** by itself prove Chef Gringo is approved to monetize that product or brand.
- Prefer problem-led product selection over broad catalog dumping.

## Future Product Harvest scoring fields

When Product Harvest is extended, candidate products should be scored on at least:

1. operator fit
2. problem severity
3. evidence quality
4. purchase intent
5. audience breadth
6. workflow fit
7. brand credibility
8. AOV / order-value potential
9. commission quality
10. attribution/cookie quality
11. reversal/return risk
12. content opportunity
13. current approved-partner status
14. current inventory / availability confidence

**Important:** customer/operator fit and evidence are evaluated before economics. Commission cannot change the recommendation score.
